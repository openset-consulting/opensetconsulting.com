Outcomes

What a cycle can target

  • An AI or technology initiative, taken to something the organization can actually use
  • Stakeholder alignment around a complex initiative
  • A stalled cross-functional project, moving toward launch again
  • The shift from doing the technical work to leading others through it
  • Technical direction and decisions people actually follow
  • Bigger work without burnout, so evenings and weekends stop being the release valve
  • Clarity on where and how they want to make their impact
Structure

The 90 day cycle

Week 1

Set the target

  • A 90 minute planning session
  • Optional 30 minutes with their manager
  • A written plan the leader owns
Weeks 2 to 11

Lead the work

  • A 60 minute session every other week
  • Close reviews of real work when it matters
  • Reachable in between
Week 12

Review, reset

  • Progress against the indicators
  • The plan updated with where it landed
  • The next outcome, or this one sharpened
Your role

Less coaching load on your calendar. More ownership in theirs.

If you want a say in the outcome

Thirty minutes in week 1 to set it with your leader, then the readout at 90 days.

If you'd rather stay out of it

Your leader sets the outcome with me. You join them for the readout at 90 days.
